UniViewer Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10
Privacy policy URL: https://globalcorehub.github.io/heic-viewer-master-support/univiewer-privacy.txt

中文

UniViewer Pro 是一款 Windows 桌面文件查看与转换工具，用于在用户设备上打开 .msg、.eml、.vcf、.ics 文件。

本地处理的数据

用户通过文件选择器、拖放、命令行或 Windows 文件关联显式选择的文件；
从这些文件中解析出的邮件头、邮件正文、联系人、日历事件和附件；
用户为导出 CSV、PDF 或批量附件提取显式选择的本地保存位置；
应用设置，例如界面语言、主题和本地 Pro 授权状态。

上述内容在本机处理，用于显示、搜索、导出和附件提取。当前代码路径未发现遥测上传、用户账号系统、广告 SDK、用户文件内容上传服务器或第三方分析服务。

本地日志

应用可在自身运行目录写入 trace.log 和 crash.log，用于本地诊断启动、解析异常与测试。日志仅保存在用户设备上，不会由应用主动上传。用户可删除这些文件。

Microsoft Store 与购买

Pro 授权通过 Windows.Services.Store.StoreContext 查询和购买。购买、付款、许可证验证、退款与相关账户数据由 Microsoft 按其服务条款和隐私政策处理。应用不收集银行卡信息、Microsoft 账户密码或账单地址。

批量导出、超过 2 个文件的批量导入和一键批量附件提取是 Pro 功能；免费版仍可查看单个文件。应用不会通过上传用户文件来判断是否允许使用功能。

文件与权限

应用使用桌面文件访问能力打开用户选择的文件，并使用文件/文件夹选择器保存导出结果。它不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录同步或后台持续采集。runFullTrust 用于 Windows 桌面文件解析和导出体验。

第三方内容

应用可显示邮件正文中的 HTML、链接和附件名称。除非用户主动点击链接或保存/打开附件，应用不会把邮件内容发送给外部服务。WebView2 呈现的本地邮件正文可能包含原邮件自带的远程图片或链接；这类请求由 Windows/WebView2 与邮件内容本身决定，用户应避免打开不受信任邮件中的外部内容。

儿童

应用面向一般办公用户，不包含用户生成内容、社交功能、广告或面向儿童的定向内容。建议年龄分级为 3+ / 所有人，但最终分级以 Partner Center 问卷为准。

联系我们

本产品不强制提供支持邮箱或 Support URL。如政策变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

English

UniViewer Pro is a Windows desktop viewer and converter for .msg, .eml, .vcf, and .ics files.

Data processed locally

Files explicitly selected by the user through a file picker, drag-and-drop, command line, or Windows file association;
Email headers, email bodies, contacts, calendar events, and attachments parsed from those files;
Local destination folders explicitly chosen for CSV export, PDF export, or batch attachment extraction;
App settings such as display language, theme, and local Pro license state.

This data is processed on the device to display, search, export, and extract attachments. The current code path contains no telemetry upload, user account system, advertising SDK, user-content upload server, or third-party analytics service.

Local logs

The app may write trace.log and crash.log in its own runtime directory for local diagnostics of startup, parsing errors, and self-tests. These logs remain on the device and are not actively uploaded by the app. Users may delete them.

Microsoft Store and purchases

Pro licensing uses Windows.Services.Store.StoreContext. Purchases, payments, license checks, refunds, and related account data are handled by Microsoft under its terms and privacy policy. The app does not collect payment-card details, Microsoft account passwords, or billing addresses.

Batch export, importing more than two files at once, and one-click batch attachment extraction are Pro features. Single-file viewing remains free. The app never uploads user files to decide feature availability.

Files and capabilities

The app uses desktop file access for user-selected files and file/folder pickers for export destinations. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contact synchronization, or continuous background collection. runFullTrust supports the Windows desktop parsing and export experience.

Third-party content

The app can display HTML bodies, links, and attachment names contained in email files. It does not send email content to external services unless the user opens a link, saves/opens an attachment, or otherwise initiates that action. WebView2 rendering of a local email body may encounter remote images or links already present in the original message; such requests are governed by Windows/WebView2 and the email content itself. Avoid opening external content in untrusted messages.

Children

The app targets general office users and contains no user-generated content, social features, ads, or child-directed content. A 3+ / Everyone rating is recommended, but the final rating depends on the Partner Center questionnaire.

Contact

A Support URL and support email are optional for this product. Policy updates will revise this page and its effective date.
