Privacy Policy — HEIC Viewer Master 1.2.0

Effective date: 7 November 2026
Publisher: GlobalCore Labs

Summary

HEIC Viewer Master is a local, offline Windows desktop image viewer and converter. All image decoding, EXIF/GPS metadata reading, histogram calculation, image conversion, watermark rendering and file saving happen on your device. The app requires no account and does not upload your photos.

Data processed on your device

The app reads the local files you open in order to display, copy or convert them. Files remain in the folders you choose. Clipboard copy and file associations stay under your control.

Optional one-time Microsoft Store durable add-on

Version 1.2.0 may offer an optional one-time Microsoft Store durable add-on for four new workflow automations. It is a one-time purchase. Features shipped in 1.1.0 remain free forever. Microsoft handles payment, licensing and purchase receipts; the app requires no account and processes no payment data.

Local entitlement cache

The app stores only {owned, productId} in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PixSwift\pro-entitlement.json. It contains no photos, names, contact details, usage history or behavioral data. You can delete it at any time and use Restore Purchases to ask the Microsoft Store again.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your photos, file names, folder paths, EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates, clipboard contents or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising SDKs, performs no behavioral analytics and includes no usage-image telemetry or social sign-in.

Microsoft Store

Installation, updates, licensing and any optional add-on purchase are handled by Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy terms.

Children

The app is a general-purpose offline image utility and is not directed to children. It contains no user-generated content, chat, social feeds or online interaction.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.
