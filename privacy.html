Privacy Policy — HEIC Viewer Master

Effective date: 5 November 2026
Publisher: GlobalCore Labs

Summary

HEIC Viewer Master is designed as a local, offline Windows desktop image viewer and converter. The application does not require an account, does not upload your photos, and does not operate a photo server.

Data processed on your device

Image decoding, EXIF/GPS metadata reading, histogram calculation, image conversion, watermark rendering and file saving are performed locally on your Windows device. Files you open remain in the folders you choose. The app reads file contents only to display or convert the image you selected.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your photos, file names, folder paths, EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates, clipboard contents or device identifiers. The app does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics or social sign-in.

Clipboard and file associations

If you explicitly use Copy, the decoded image is placed in the Windows clipboard on your device. If Windows file associations are enabled, double-clicking a supported image file launches the app with that local file path. Both actions stay under your control.

Microsoft Store

Installation and updates are handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's own privacy terms. The current version is free and has no in-app purchases; we do not receive payment card details.

Children

The app is a general-purpose offline image utility and is not directed to children. It contains no user-generated content, chat, social feeds or online interaction.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
